# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults using Carin to record foods and health measurements and understand nutrition information from the data they provide. Registration is limited to ages 18–50 inclusive. Health-profile data is optional and can be completed later.

## Product Purpose

Carin helps people keep food-consumption records, review supported nutrition details, record measurements, and see relevant follow-up information. It makes the distinction between analyzing food before eating and recording food already consumed explicit. Carin supports understanding and record keeping; it does not diagnose, triage, prescribe, or replace professional care.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, reassuring. The intended experience is a cozy nutrition companion: encouraging without judgment, clear without sounding clinical, and light in tone without hiding uncertainty.

## Anti-references

- Do not imitate calorie-goal dashboards, calorie adequacy rings, scores, streaks, or meal timelines unless Carin's product contract later supports them.
- Do not use diagnosis, disease-risk scores, medication changes, or claims that a result is clinically normal.
- Avoid a dashboard that feels like an undifferentiated stack of nested rounded cards, repeated decorative icon tiles, or mascot decoration without a useful role.
- Do not use color alone to imply a good/normal result or make missing data look like zero.

## Design Principles

- Keep planned analysis distinct from confirmed consumption.
- Show data coverage, missing values, source, and uncertainty honestly.
- Use a warm, playful voice to encourage, never to judge or overclaim.
- Spend visual emphasis on the next useful action and the information the user came to inspect.
- Use the mascot for context, guidance, or reassurance, not as repeated decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text, use descriptive accessible names and heading semantics, provide at least 44 dp touch targets, support system text scaling, and pair color with labels or icons. Motion must communicate state, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never be required to understand content.
